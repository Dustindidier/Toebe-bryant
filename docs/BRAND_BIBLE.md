# Brand Bible — Toebe Bryant

## Pitch
**Toebe Bryant: The GOAT of Feet** — an anonymous adult male foot creator treating his launch as a legendary NBA debut. The seriousness of the sports broadcast is the joke; real feet and a respectful customer experience are the business.

## Approved positioning
- Name: Toebe Bryant. Anonymous, faceless, adult male; all fans welcome.
- Tone: funny, cocky, basketball-literate, confident, subtly seductive, never embarrassed.
- Primary tagline: **No face. All game.**
- Supporting: **The GOAT of Feet**, **Hall of Fame soles**, **Shoot your shot**, **Mamba Mentality. Barefoot Reality.** (last line has IP/publicity risk).
- Real content: natural/unpedicured feet, soles, arches, close-ups, ordinary socks and sneakers, approved custom requests.
- Character: over-the-top franchise player; fake scouting reports, rookie debut, player stats, arena introductions.
- Palette: purple, black, gold, white; original court/tunnel/broadcast graphics.
- Do not disclose face, real name, location, home, employer, family or identifying details.

## Rejected
- “Ten toes. One questionable career decision.” (self-deprecating/shame tone).
- Generic influencer voice, purely erotic or overly formal copy.
- Previous guessed Higgsfield casting (Holden/Desmond/Grady) was rejected as “way off.” Final voices unselected.
